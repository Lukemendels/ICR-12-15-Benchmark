# Adversarial challenge outcome

The round began after two consecutive saturated batches at 69 reviews. Four deliberately selected collections were then reviewed, bringing the evidence base to 73 unique OMB controls across 38 agency/component labels.

| Challenge | Result | Effect on conclusions |
|---|---|---|
| FinCEN SAR, 202405-1506-005 | 68. The 2020 method includes declined cases, filing modes, complexity and occupational time mixes. The 2024 renewal carries forward a rounded average and old labor prices. It discusses a conflicting 15-large-bank survey rather than ignoring it. | Strong transferable methods outside TSA; current application illustrates why updating a population alone does not update a model. Existing segmentation, nonreporter and validation entities suffice. |
| OSHA process safety, 202508-1218-005 | 80. Rich activity/occupation segmentation and exact activity-level change bridge; administrative coverage adjustments; stale response-column entries and inherited timing assumptions. | A particularly strong TSA-relevant regulatory comparator. Its two-point advantage over the earlier OSHA comparator is not treated as a material ranking difference. No federal dimension leader changes. |
| EPA chemical reporting, 202401-2070-005 | 76. Explicit four-year reporting cycles prorated across a three-year approval period; fixed/variable federal costs; old/new driver bridge. This recent nonsubstantive package retains a 2023 supporting analysis. | Useful application of existing horizon, cohort and baseline entities. It contains a 6,000-hour summary typo and a federal unit-time mismatch. |
| NASS agricultural surveys, 202603-0535-001 | 75. Separate advance materials, nonresponse, testing, survey waves and program changes; embedded tables rendered and transcribed. | Repeated statistical-agency evidence does not establish that all surveys are superior. The federal budget lacks workload detail and the sample total needs clarification. |

The challenge did not reveal a missing major dimension, materially different canonical architecture, new authoritative source category, or significant new software requirement. It did strengthen counterevidence to TSA uniqueness. Saturation streak remains two; no automatic freeze was taken before validation.

The separate calibration analysis in methodology/calibration.md removes Item13/14 scores as a sensitivity test, examines high-complexity close comparators, and separates document length from rigor. The broad conclusion survives: TSA has strong methods and inconsistent public reconstruction quality; best practice is distributed across agencies. Public records cannot establish undisclosed internal analysis or the cause of TSA's investments.
