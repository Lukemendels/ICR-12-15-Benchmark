# Calibration and comparison limits

Rubric1.0.0 retains all nine user-specified weights. No empirical finding required reweighting. Scores measure the accessible reconstruction chain, including specifically cited accessible supplements, rather than institutional reputation, document length or OMB approval. Scores were reviewed by one analyst; no inter-rater reliability claim is made. One- or two-point differences should not be interpreted as meaningful superiority.

The calibration re-examined BLS SOII, BLS NCS, EBSA settlement, OSHA extinguishers and process safety, FCC IPCS, TSA aircraft operators and flight training, and the direct Coast Guard/CMMC challengers against the same anchors. Full reproducibility requires more than multiplying an asserted total; source provenance and assumptions remain separate dimensions. Conversely, an unexplained federal budget should not invalidate correctly exposed respondent arithmetic. No score changes were necessary. BLS SOII's ECEC dollar32.91 was verified against the September2023 release; its statement mislabels the reference period as December2023. This resolved an evidence question without removing the documented citation defect.

Zero costs can be legitimate and receive full credit. They are not exemplars of nonzero capital estimation. Federal incremental-cost exclusions and budget allocations are assessed on their stated scope and evidence, not on the magnitude of the total. A new collection may justify a zero prior inventory but still needs a gross/new change bridge; lack of a previous collection is not itself a weakness.

Complexity categories are qualitative: low means a few homogeneous tasks, medium means multiple activities or paths, high means substantial segmentation or cost structure, and very high means extensive regulatory or calibrated models. Original labels are retained; moderate was normalized to medium. TSA relevance direct/high was normalized to high; adjacent to medium; method transfer to technique. This metadata harmonization changes no scores.

Sensitivity records: data/score-sensitivity.csv. Agency means and dispersion: data/agency-summary.csv. Most agencies have too few heterogeneous purposive observations to establish agency-wide quality or consistency. Even TSA's ten observations are not a random or matched sample. Findings should be reported by method and collection, with agency summaries explicitly descriptive.
