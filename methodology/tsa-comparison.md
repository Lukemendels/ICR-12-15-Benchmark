# TSA gap analysis

Ten TSA collections score 50–76, mean 67.4, under rubric1.0.0. The best supported characterization is **inconsistent public reconstruction quality, with several strong methods**. This purposive sample cannot establish that TSA is typical or unusually rigorous across the federal population. It does not support unique or universal best-in-class practice. Other collections equal or exceed TSA in meaningful dimensions, including close security and workplace-compliance comparators.

TSA flight training scores52/60 on the Item12 composite, close to BLS SOII/NCS at53. TSA aircraft operators, airports and TWIC provide useful activity and pathway models. Gaps recur in assumption support, occupation-to-task matching, fees versus resources, precision and Item15 driver bridges.
