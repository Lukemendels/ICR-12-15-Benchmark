# Governing instructions and analytical enhancements
Verified sources: SRC-0001 (current-hosted general instructions,undated) and SRC-0002 (ROCIS PRA Module User Guide,v9.0,June2026); current 5CFR1320.3 and1320.8 and OIRA-maintained Digital.gov burden guidance supplement these.

## Required PRA compliance and submission instructions
- Item12: respondents,frequency,annualhours,estimationmethod;separate forms;appropriate laborcategories. Instructions call for ranges and explanation when burdens vary widely. Consultation is desirable;do not commission a special survey solely for estimates unless directed.
- Item13: annual non-hour costs,capital/startup annualized over useful life,separate O&M/services;methods,life,discount rate andcost timing whereapplicable;explain variation. Exclude alreadycounted labor andusual/customary ornon-informationcollection compliance costs.
- Item14: annualized federal costs andmethod,including quantified hours andincremental operating expenses. A program's entire budget is not automatically a collection cost.
- Item15: explain programchanges andadjustments. ROCIS separately records changes attributable to statute,agency discretion,estimate adjustment andpotential PRA violations. June2026 guide pp49–51 defines adjustments as changes notresulting from deliberate federal action.
- 5CFR1320.8(a)(4) requires a specific objectively supported estimate. Evidentiary support is not merely optional analytical sophistication.

## Enhanced analytical practice
Reproducible formula files;atomic activities andlineage;input-level source dates/identifiers;automatic dimensional checks;complete old-to-new driver decomposition;explicit uncertainty commensurate with significance;versioned price andpolicy baselines;separate social-resource costs from fees/transfers andbudget effects;source-aware deterministic tables andprose.
These are enhancements to documentation,reusability andauditability. They must not be misrepresented as a uniform statutory requirement for a particular software format.

## Instruction defects and interpretation register
Both SRC-0001 Item12 and SRC-0002 appendix route outside-party costs to federal cost in wording that conflicts with Item13's explicit inclusion of purchased informationcollection services. Operational ROCIS cost-burden instructions andthe economic identity ofthe payer support distinguishing respondent-paid services inItem13 from government-paid contracts inItem14. Preserve this as an interpretation,not a silently altered quotation;confirm with agency PRA/OIRA guidance before hardcoding.
The June2026 appendix also gives the optional combined table sentence its own number15,shifting the change question to16. Use the semantic Items12–15 convention inthis mission;record the source numbering defect so citations do not drift.
PRA burden scope anddownstream regulatory cost scope are related butnot identical. The tool needs separate ledgers andexplicit mapping;do not treat all compliance expenditures orfees as equivalent welfare costs.
