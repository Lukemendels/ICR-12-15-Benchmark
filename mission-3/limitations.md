# Final limitations — M3-1.0.0

- Findings describe public published estimation evidence. Public evidence cannot reveal undocumented internal production causes. Absence of a public explanation does not prove absence of an internal rationale.
- The 90 recent TSA packages are versions in the fixed 2021-09-09 through 2026-09-09 search window, not 90 independent collections or currently approved estimates. The inventory records active, pending, historical and improperly submitted/proposed packages separately. There are 48 assigned control histories and five unassigned-control packages; do not add these as equivalent denominators.
- Every recent version has principal-task normalization and Items 12–15 section coverage. This is not exhaustive scalar reconstruction of every model. There are 468 normalized activity representations plus 25 assumption-context activity nodes; overlapping source representations and serial versions must not be summed. Semantic normalization does not make unlike tasks comparable.
- Strong/moderate similarity is bounded to the named component. Unknown task scope, actor, annual/per-event denominator, statistic or lifecycle prevents an automatic numerical comparison. CONSISTENT means published coefficient/method agreement, not empirical validation of actual burden.
- All 84 recent packages with explicit previous links reach usable statements; six have no previous link. One administrative predecessor has no statement and is retained in its chain. Prior retrieval does not verify every Item 15 numerical baseline. Only the registered TWIC bridge is fully verified here; unavailable internal workbooks and administrative evidence constrain further reconciliation.
- The 162 reviewed assumption observations comprise 69 scalar/categorical assumptions and 93 formula-header coefficients. Lexical discovery across 17 families is broader than reviewed evidence (16 families among reviewed assumption nodes). A discovery hit, missing edge or missing observation is not an adjudication, a zero, or proof of absence.
- The 341 quantitative checks cover 40 versions and include row screens and 41 reused Mission 1 checks. They are not 341 independent findings or complete model validation for all 90 packages. Eight major within-ICR findings are adjudicated separately. No portfolio error prevalence or office ranking follows.
- Original source values and source precision are retained; reconstructed products do not establish the correct underlying behavioral assumption or an aggregate corrected burden/cost. Earlier recurrence of a value does not establish its internal production mechanism.
- The graph preserves exact archived text/geometry and frozen-record locators. Raw source bytes are not all duplicated. Locator validation confirms archived resolution and content hashes, not permanent live availability of public URLs or truth of empirical assumptions.
- Federal comparators are the four already selected frozen Mission 1 methodological examples. They do not establish TSA uniqueness or justify transferring federal coefficients to unlike TSA tasks.
