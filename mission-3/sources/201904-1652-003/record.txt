Abstract:
TSA created a mandatory security program for all-cargo aircraft operations and amending existing security regulations and programs for aircraft operators, foreign air carriers, airport operators, and indirect air carriers (IACs). TSA also conducts security threat assessments on individiuals, including IAC employees who have unescorted access to cargo.

Short Statement:
TSA has eliminated a requirement that foreign air carriers submit quarterly reports to TSA’s Global Strategies office. This has resulted in a decreased hour burden of 520 hours. These carriers are still required to respond to other parts of this information collection so there is no change in the number of respondents as a result of this change.
