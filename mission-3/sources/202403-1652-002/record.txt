Abstract:
TSA’s Enhancing Surface Cyber Risk Management (CRM) Notice of Proposed Rulemaking (NPRM) codifies the cybersecurity requirements in these SDs along with additional requirements, and reorganizes these requirements to align with the Cross-Sector Cybersecurity Performance Goals (CPGs) developed by the Cybersecurity Infrastructure and Security Agency (CISA) . The CRM NPRM addresses the pervasive cybersecurity threats to the Nation’s most critical pipeline, freight, and public transportation and passenger rail infrastructure.
