Abstract:
The Implementing Recommendations of the 9/11 Commission Act of 2007 (9/11 Act) specifically requires TSA to set regulatory standards for mandatory security threat assessments (STAs) of certain freight railroad carrier, public transportation and passenger railroad (PTPR) agency, and over-the-road bus (OTRB) owner/operator employees and prohibits entities from employing individuals in specified positions unless they have successfully completed the STA process. TSA collects information to meet its statutory obligation to codify security vetting requirements.
