## Nuances and sensitive wording

Use “published values do not independently reconcile,” “public documentation does not establish the reason,” and “the difference remains unresolved from published evidence.” Absence of a published rationale is not evidence that no internal rationale exists. Preserve approved, active, pending and proposed distinctions. Method agreement is component-specific and does not prove that a coefficient measures actual performance. Explained variation is not endorsement of every proxy. Assumption observations include formula headers and repeated versions; their counts are not independent validated defaults.
