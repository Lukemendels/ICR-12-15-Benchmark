# Normalization rules

Preserve verbatim evidence and published numbers. Convert hours to minutes by multiplication by 60; retain source precision and both values. Do not infer active versus elapsed time when the source does not say. Annual response counts differ from unique people/entities; preserve event units and population overlap. One-time implementation and recurring steady-state work are distinct.

Keep wage statistics, occupation/SOC, industry/NAICS, geography, wage year and loading-source date. Direct total compensation, total-compensation/wage ratios, wage/(1-benefit-share), benefit/wage markups and overhead are distinct methods. Do not inflation-adjust wages without an explicit index and rationale. Nominal vintage differences alone cannot establish methodological inconsistency.

Record source-stated and calculated results separately. Null means unknown/not extracted; zero means an explicit source statement with scope. Preserve gross resource cost, respondent fee, Federal fee offset and net budget cost separately. Approval horizons, reporting periods and capital life are different fields.

Item 15 baseline identities distinguish prior approval, prior statement, public notice, withdrawn request and transferred burden. No cross-version bridge is called verified merely because two displayed totals subtract correctly.
