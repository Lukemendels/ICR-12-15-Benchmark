# Inspectable activity similarity

Candidate discovery uses controlled activity labels; labels alone never authorize a numeric finding. Final groups inspect actor, operational obligation, task scope, mode, recurrence, lifecycle, population unit, role, complexity, security context, measurement basis and vintage.

Strong: matching actor and substantive task boundary with no known material scope conflict. Moderate: related task and useful method comparison, with meaningful scope differences or incomplete documentation. Weak: thematic relationship useful for discovery only. Unknown fields reduce strength; they are not matching evidence.

Sequence: identify comparable activity → observe difference → test explanatory features → assess cited explanation → classify. CONSISTENT is local to the tested method/value and versions. DIFFERENT_EXPLAINED requires observable support. POTENTIALLY_INCONSISTENT requires useful comparability and an unexplained material difference after challenge. NOT_COMPARABLE records a failed match. UNRESOLVED means evidence cannot settle scope or explanation. No automatic severity or office ranking.

Time comparisons check scope, active/elapsed, recurring/one-time, actor and statistic. Population comparisons check entity/person/event units and overlap. Labor comparisons check task occupation, statistic, vintage, industry, geography and loading base. Cost comparisons check payer, resource/transfer and annualization. Item 15 requires baseline identity. Every retained comparison records these tests and caveats.
