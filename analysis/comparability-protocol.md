# Final comparability contract

Compare source-defined components before numeric values. Record actor, respondent/population, legal or operational requirement, frequency, recurrence, interaction mode, lifecycle, complexity, occupation, technology, evidence basis, vintage and implementation status. Unknown features are missing evidence, never positive matches.

Strong matching permits a bounded component comparison; moderate matching permits method review with explicit scope caveats; weak matching is discovery only. Preserve failed matches as NOT_COMPARABLE and incomplete scope as UNRESOLVED. Classifications concern the named dimensions and versions, not whole ICR quality. Serial versions and transferred requirements are not independent replications.

The canonical adjudication ledger is `analysis/adjudicated-comparisons.jsonl`. `analysis/comparisons.jsonl` remains the inherited Batch 13 evidence. Each canonical record contains member observations with original and normalized values/units/periods, source provenance, explanation tests, classification, confidence and practitioner significance. Discovery hits are not reviewed assumption observations.

Within-ICR arithmetic uses a separate QA ledger. Numeric disagreement alone does not create a cross-ICR inconsistency. Source rounding is evaluated in its stated units; source totals are never overwritten. Major candidates must survive source, lineage, related-task, population, period, vintage and comparability challenge. Do not infer internal causes.

Stability is evaluated against schema, activity/assumption taxonomies, similarity and comparison rules, classifications and major findings. Inherited Batch 13's stability statement does not by itself establish the required two stable final batches.
