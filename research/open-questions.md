# Unresolved questions at Mission1 freeze

These questions were reviewed after saturation and challenge; they remain limitations rather than reasons to repeat ordinary ICR discovery.

| Question | Disposition and evidence needed |
|---|---|
| Did the recent TSA process overhaul affect public estimates? | Unresolved. The August21/September1,2026 submissions are temporally relevant, but adoption requires verified process/model history. |
| Is TSA unusually rigorous across the federal population? | Not estimable from a purposive sample. A representative or carefully matched study and independent scoring are needed. |
| Are internal models stronger than statements suggest? | Public reconstruction quality is the measured outcome; internal files or controlled replication access would be needed. |
| Which current capital model is fully defensible? | Historical EPA annuity methods are explicit, but current inputs, cohorts and PRA scope remain case-specific. No universal recent winner. |
| Which overhead method should TSA use? | Preserve direct ECEC, ratio/share loading and documented overhead alternatives. Select by cost scope; replicate EBSA allocation before adoption. |
| Can statistical models be fully reconstructed publicly? | IRS coefficients/microdata are not available in reviewed packages. A synthetic example or controlled-access protocol would help. |
| How should inconsistent instruction wording be implemented? | Semantic payer/item mapping is documented. Resolve purchased-service routing and numbering with agency PRA/OIRA guidance before software defaults. |
| Are all rows immediately reusable in software? | No. Structured models, Word cells, workbook formulas/caches and original evidence are preserved. Peripheral semantic normalization remains an implementation task. |
| Are identified differences actual errors? | Some are arithmetic conflicts; others may reflect hidden precision, scope or versions. Preserve agency values and cite exact evidence before publication. |

See research/limitations.md, methodology/calibration.md and research/final-validation.json. Mission2 may conduct narrowly targeted clarification and version any new evidence; broad discovery is not the default next action.
