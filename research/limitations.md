# Limitations retained at evidence freeze

1. The sample is purposive and selected for information gain. It supports discovery and comparison of observed methods, not estimates of the prevalence of quality across government. Related controls, such as the two CMMC packages, are not independent agency replications.
2. The study evaluates accessible public documentation. A weak statement may conceal a strong internal model; a detailed statement may rest on weak assumptions. No inference about staff competence, internal review quality or causal institutional motivation is warranted.
3. Scores are single-analyst judgments under a transparent rubric. They are not validated psychometric measurements. Close scores are effectively tied for interpretation. No agency-wide best or most-consistent designation is established.
4. The window is based on actual OIRA receipt/submission dates. Some packages retain older statements, and some pending requests were not approved at retrieval. Package status, document dates and receipt dates are separate fields. Historical methods are identified and not counted as separate recent ICRs.
5. TSA's surface cybersecurity request was submitted September1,2026; HME August21,2026, with its statement dated August20. Both were received in OIRA, not concluded, at retrieval. They fall within the approximately June9–September9 period implied by the user's reported overhaul. No public evidence establishes that either used the new internal process. Reference prefixes beginning202606 are not submission dates. No before/after causal evaluation is available.
6. Original statements, accessible supplements and selected underlying sources are archived. Not every citation, administrative query, vendor quote, internal pay table, proprietary study or restricted model coefficient was independently obtained. Missing inputs are recorded; no gap is imputed as zero. Source access status distinguishes archival originals from extracted web verification and inaccessible binaries.
7. The structured evidence captures the analytical model and performed checks. Word tables and available workbook cells/formulas preserve additional rows. It is not a fully executable replica of all73 models. Some peripheral rows require semantic normalization before application reuse. A reproducible benchmark dataset does not imply that every agency estimate passed reconstruction.
8. Differences between reported and recomputed values are evidence of a reconstruction question, not automatically a proved agency error. Hidden precision, rounding, scope, version changes and transcription can matter. Significant findings should cite the exact source rows and preserve the agency's value alongside the independent calculation.
9. PRA scope, regulatory resource cost, fees, transfers, government budget and financing are not interchangeable. Current instructions also contain a purchased-service routing conflict and a question-numbering issue. The future tool should retain semantic item identity and require authoritative resolution before hardcoding disputed defaults.
10. No recent ICR in this sample is certified as a universally complete capital-cost exemplar. Historical EPA methods show explicit principal/life/discount formulas, but current prices, cohorts and PRA mapping still need validation. Likewise, strong federal workload examples do not establish full reconstruction of all internal program costs.

These limits were reviewed after saturation and the adversarial round. Additional ordinary ICRs are unlikely to resolve the principal remaining gaps, which concern unavailable internal evidence, representative sampling, independent scoring, or interpretation of cost scope. Mission2 should preserve these limits and avoid converting qualified findings into categorical agency rankings.
