Provisional review. 202310-1220-004; sources SRC-0078, SRC-0077.

Score 78/100. Public documentation only. extensive statement; high complexity.

## Scoring

- reproducibility: 19. Year, sector, contact type, response frequency and minutes are tabulated; averages use rounded annual totals.

- provenance: 11. March 2023 ECEC and historical collection experience identified; citation filename and underlying timing studies require verification.

- segmentation: 15. Initiation, update, nonresponse, testing and quality recontacts are distinct, with public/private strata.

- labor: 8. Direct ECEC compensation avoids a second benefits load; historical 70/30 occupational mix disclosed.

- item13: 8. Explicitly excludes capital, O&M and purchased services because only respondent time is used.

- item14: 3. Only collection/noncollection budget split, without workload or allocation derivation.

- item15: 6. Prior approval and new total identified; increased sample explains direction but no quantitative driver bridge.

- validation: 4. Past collection experience supports timing and mix; specific timing-study evidence not supplied.

- consistency: 4. Annualization and wage products reconcile within rounding; unique respondents versus repeated QA contacts requires care.

## Reconstruction issues

- Item 14 budget split cannot independently reconstruct federal resource quantities or allocations.

- Item 15 does not quantify separate effects of sample size, response rates and cohort composition.

## Methods worth testing

- Count time spent by nonrespondents and quality-assurance recontacts.

- Use observed occupational mix and direct total compensation rather than an unexplained wage markup.
