## Reconstruction issues

- Prose says transaction burden ranges up to six hours, while medical appeal denial uses 8.67 hours.

- Agency explicitly puts purchased third-party administration in Item 12 hours; classification differs from the purchased-service convention and needs guidance reconciliation.

- Tables 1 and 3 use inconsistent approved/denied/extended labels for some disability transactions.

## Methods worth testing

- Separate unique entities, transactions, outcome branches and delivery modes.

- Record the agency cost-classification convention independently of economic labor and purchased-service quantities.
