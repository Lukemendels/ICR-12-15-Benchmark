Provisional review. 202506-0938-007; sources SRC-0090, SRC-0089.

Score 60/100. Public documentation only. moderate statement; high complexity.

## Scoring

- reproducibility: 17. Hospital count and two activity/occupation groups reconstruct hours and labor cost.

- provenance: 9. Dated STAR registry and 2023 OEWS occupation inputs; burden hours inherited.

- segmentation: 10. Reporting and recordkeeping separated, but hospital complexity variation not modeled.

- labor: 4. Occupation match and doubling stated; 100% benefit/overhead markup is not derived.

- item13: 4. Capital zero asserted without discussion of O&M and cost-report software.

- item14: 4. MAC and CMS totals supplied without hours, rates or allocation methodology.

- item15: 7. Old/new hospital counts, time and cost shown, with transfers from two controls; transfer-to-average-hour derivation unclear.

- validation: 3. Administrative population count supported; 675 hours lacks measured timing validation.

- consistency: 2. Main products reconcile; transferred requirement counts do not directly explain one additional hour for all hospitals.

## Reconstruction issues

- Federal contractor and CMS totals are not independently reconstructable.
