1. Is the proposed collection of information necessary for the NRC to properly perform its
functions? Does the information have practical utility? Please explain your response.
Yes, the proposed information collection is necessary for the NRC to perform its functions
properly. Yes, the information has practical utility because it enables the NRC to use the
information and assess the safety of a reactor.
